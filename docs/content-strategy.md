# Content Strategy

## Positioning

The portfolio is written in English for publisher-facing use. The core message is:

Irem Salk and Eralp Ozer have shipped and contributed to many mobile game projects across casual, puzzle, sorting, jam, runner, and hybrid-casual formats, with the ability to move from ideas to playable store-ready products.

## Featured Project Criteria

Featured projects are selected manually in `FEATURED_NAMES` inside `web/scripts/build_portfolio_data.py`.

Current featured list:

- Shopping Jam
- Perfect Pour 3D
- Merge and Spin
- Match Stackers
- Block Quest Jam
- Liquid Links

The current criteria are:

- Has usable screenshots.
- Looks strong in a publisher portfolio.
- Gives a balanced view across Irem's App Store work and Eralp's Google Play partner work.
- Includes projects with meaningful iteration/version signal when available.

If the user wants specific games featured, update `FEATURED_NAMES` and rerun the pipeline.

## Catalog Strategy

All normalized games are shown in the complete catalog. Non-featured games are intentionally compact so the page keeps momentum while still proving breadth.

Available filters:

- All
- Featured
- App Store
- Google Play
- Irem
- Eralp
- 2026
- 2025
- 2024
- 2023

The search box matches game name, platform, seller, year, genre, and contributor names.

## Tone

The copy should remain practical and publisher-oriented. Avoid exaggerated claims, unverified revenue or KPI statements, and anything that implies ownership beyond the roles listed by the user.
